Research
6 areas · 1 platform

My work centers on what happens in the microseconds after a nanosecond laser pulse strikes a thin metallic film — melting, evaporation, plasma formation, and the redeposition that follows — studied through an automated pump–probe system I designed and built.

Featured Platform
Built from the ground up

Automated Pump–Probe Diagnostic System

A modular optical system for studying front- and rear-side ablation of thin films using a transparent substrate. A CW probe laser continuously monitors transmission and reflection through a single ablation event, avoiding the need for repeated delay-scanned shots that conventional pump–probe methods require.

The full control, acquisition, and analysis stack — synchronization, motorized X–Z positioning, and single-pulse triggering — is released as open software.

Pump laserNd:YAG, 1064 nm, ~10 ns
