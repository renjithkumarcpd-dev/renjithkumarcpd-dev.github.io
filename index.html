Current Research Interests
Where the experiment meets the model

Alongside the pump–probe measurements, I'm extending the picture computationally — connecting what the diagnostics observe to hydrodynamic and thermal models of the ablation process.

FLASH · hydro

Hydrodynamic Simulation of Laser-Produced Plasma

Working with the FLASH radiation-hydrodynamics code to reproduce and extend recent double-front plasma modelling of nanosecond laser ablation, with the aim of capturing front- and rear-side ablation of thin metallic films on quartz substrates.

COMSOL · thermal

Modelling Post-Ablation Melt Dynamics

Building models of the melt film that persists after ablation — incorporating evaporative loss, disjoining pressure, and capillary flow — to connect surface morphology and redeposition back to what the pump–probe platform measures.

Featured Platform
Built from the ground up

Automated Pump–Probe Diagnostic System

A modular pump–probe system for studying front- and rear-side ablation of thin films using a transparent substrate. A CW probe laser monitors the temporal evolution of the ablation site through transmission and reflection measurements.

The system integrates synchronization, motorized sample positioning, single-pulse control, data acquisition, and analysis.

Pump laserNd:YAG, 1064 nm, ~10 ns
Probe laserCW DPSS, 532 nm
Fluence rangeup to ~15 J/cm²
Pressure range0.01–1000 mbar
DetectorAvalanche photodiode
